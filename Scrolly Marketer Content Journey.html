02 · WHERE WE STARTED

Past

Fragmented effort limited our ability to move with confidence.

Not long ago, making content meant agencies. A brief went out, and came back about three weeks later.

~21 days
agency turnaround, program assessment
“It takes so much time to create, and then get approved. Would love to be able to do it myself.”

The agencies did the work, and the marketers waited for the review. Marketers owned the schedule, but not much of the creation.

03 · HOW WE WORK TODAY

Present

A connected foundation is making the work clearer and more repeatable.

Then the work came home. Planning moved into the CEP, marketers started drafting in Composer, and reviews began to tier themselves.

The surveys suggest ownership went up.

54% → 64%
feeling in control · Feb 2025 to May 2026, n=136
35% → 62%
feeling like the creator · same surveys
“Doing the first email is exciting. But the sixteenth? I don’t like doing the footnotes. Is this really the best use of my talents?”
Advance Teams interview, 2025

The number of tools went up too. And so did the number of jobs one marketer holds.

“We haven’t been upskilled to be creative marketers, yet forced to become creatives, copywriters, designers, strategists all in one.”
System 1.0 survey, open text, Apr 2026

We gained control. We also took on the drafting, the references, the checking and the chasing that the agency used to do.

1.4 / 5
transformation score · 9 of 12 capabilities
“It’s been a lot of yes-and: do everything you were doing before, and now also do Aurora.”

04 · WHERE WE ARE GOING target state

Future

High capability and quality combine with lower turnaround and unit cost. Marketers keep ownership, with fewer handoffs, steps and tools.

What’s next is to fix the pain that is still there. Fewer tools and less manual work for the marketers, while keeping the creation and the capabilities in-house. Hiding the complexity behind one system.

“Good marketing starts with good creative.”
System 1.0 survey, open text, Apr 2026
“When you own the strategy, the content, the messaging and the execution, you own the end to end.”
System 1.0 survey, open text, Apr 2026
“I would like more creativity and a faster process.”
Ways-of-Working survey, open text

Handoffs happen inside the system instead of between people. Less time, and better quality content.

4.5 / 5
transformation score, target · 12 of 12 capabilities

The target is three quarters of a marketer’s time on strategy. That’s the number we will measure against.
